English Practice Notes
1. Basic Sentences
Hello, my name is Alice.
I am learning English every day.
Practice makes progress.
Reading, speaking, listening, and writing are all important.
2. Useful Phrases
Good morning.
How are you?
Nice to meet you.
Could you please repeat that?
Thank you for your help.
See you tomorrow.
3. Short Paragraph
Learning English is a long journey. At first, it may feel difficult, but small daily practice can make a big difference. Read a little, speak a little, and write a little every day. Over time, your vocabulary will grow and your confidence will improve.

5. Mini Dialogue
A: Hello! How are you today?
B: I am fine, thank you. How about you?
A: I am doing well. Are you studying English?
B: Yes, I practice English every day.
A: Great! Keep going.
